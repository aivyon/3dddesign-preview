This project is a fluid merge between indoors and outdoors activities at each level of the structure. One can feel there
is a continuous flow between the outside and inside spaces.
It is based on the notion of layers, similar to onion, and unites to architectural forms into a single organic structure.
All this without compromising the autonomy of each function.
The project is proposed to be located in the midst of central park in Manhattan New York.
(Designed by Yonatan Aminetzach)
